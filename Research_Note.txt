Research Note — Learner Problem + Product Direction 
 
Learner problem 
I have noticed that practicing LLD can be a little difficult for learners because usually we either read solutions or discuss designs without getting much feedback on our own thinking. 
For example, I can design a Parking Lot system, but after writing my classes and relationships, I may not know whether my design is actually good or where I made mistakes. 
So, I wanted to build something where a learner can: 
Pick an LLD problem. 
Write their own solution. 
Get feedback on their design. 
See what they did well and what could be improved. 
Try again after understanding the feedback. 
 
Product direction 
The main idea is to make LLD practice more interactive instead of just reading solutions. 
For the MVP, I kept the scope fairly small. The user can choose a problem, create an attempt, write their design in different sections, submit it, and get feedback. 
I also wanted the feedback to be useful rather than just giving a score. That is why the result includes things like what was done well, concerns, and suggestions. 
Later, this could be expanded with more LLD problems, better progress tracking, saved attempts, and different ways of getting feedback.
